### **Time limits**

##### **Skymarathon 43km:** 

Checkpoint 1 - Chopok approx. 23 km - CUT-OFF time - 11:20 AM / 4:20 hours from start /

Checkpoint 2 - Biela Púť approx. 32 km - CUT-OFF time - 1:15 PM / 6:15 hours from start /

##### **Skyrace 23km:** 

Checkpoint 2 - Biela Púť - CUT-OFF time - 1:00 PM / 3:00 hours from start /

Both checkpoints are located at cable car stations. In case you miss the time limit, the cable car will transport you back to the start area.  
The cable car is free of charge for competitors.
