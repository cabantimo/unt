### **Rules**
 
##### **GENERAL RULES**  

**To participate in our Marathon and Skyrace races, all competitors must:**
- be thoroughly prepared and fully understand the physical demands, distance, and duration of the event
- possess a high level of mountain self-sufficiency to independently manage challenges that may arise along the route, including:
   - handling harsh weather conditions (such as high altitude, severe winds, cold temperatures, fog, rain, hail..) without external assistance
   - managing severe physical or mental fatigue, digestive problems, muscle or joint distress, and other minor injuries
- recognize that the organizers are not responsible for helping participants overcome the aforementioned physical or logistical challenges, and that personal safety depends entirely on each runner’s ability to adapt to unexpected circumstances
- acknowledge that prior experience in similar races is beneficial, but not solely sufficient for completing this event

**Participants must :**  
- refrain from leaving litter anywhere along the course (waste must be disposed of exclusively at aid stations/checkpoints)  
- complete the entire official route in sequential order without taking shortcuts (unannounced monitoring points will be placed along the course to enforce this)  
- avoid pushing, obstructing, or causing harm to other runners, race volunteers, or spectators  
- provide immediate aid to anyone in distress on the course (runners, staff, or bystanders)  
- carry all mandatory equipment described in the "Mandatory Equipment" section throughout the entire race

##### **ADDITIONAL IMPORTANT RULES**

- Competitors must strictly comply with all time limits at designated checkpoints. If a runner decides to withdraw (DNF), they MUST immediately notify the nearest checkpoint marshal or call the organizer number to avoid unnecessary search and rescue operations.
- Your race number must be clearly visible on your body at all times and cannot be transferred to another person without official registration change.
- Outside help, pacing by non-registered runners, or receiving support outside official aid stations is strictly prohibited.
- Poles are allowed. However, if you choose to use them, you must carry them with you from start to finish. Receiving poles during the race or discarding/handing them off along the route is strictly prohibited.

###### **The organizers reserve the right to modify the course layout, time cutoffs, and checkpoint locations when necessary. Any updates will be communicated to competitors in a timely manner.**   
  

##### **SAFETY**  

The course follows technical terrain, presenting inherent risks of severe injury or death typical of high-mountain endurance events. Potential hazards include, but are not limited to: collisions with motor vehicles, obstacles, rocks, trees, or other people; falls and slips; acute physical exertion injuries; severe weather hazards such as lightning, hail, blizzards, or forest fires; drowning; interactions with wildlife, insects, or reptiles; and medical conditions including hypothermia, heatstroke, dehydration, altitude sickness, or complications arising from extreme physical strain.

By entering the race, competitors explicitly acknowledge these hazards and assume full personal responsibility for their participation, safety, and actions under all event conditions.  
In light of these factors, all participants are advised to secure personal accident and rescue insurance prior to the event (the organizers do not provide individual insurance coverage).  
Read more about mountain sports insurance here: [Alpenverein - Leitner Sport](https://www.leitnersport.sk/alpenverein)

##### **REGISTRATION TRANSFER**  
- The entry fee is non-refundable. However, if a runner cannot participate due to medical reasons, their registration fee can be transferred to the following year, provided it is reported via email prior to the event.

##### **PRIZES**  
- A prize of €500 is awarded for breaking the course record on any route.
- The top three finishers in each category (provided the category was opened) receive valuable non-monetary prizes.
