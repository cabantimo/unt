### **Location and logistics**
 
**ATTENTION: THE EVENT TAKES PLACE ON THE SOUTHERN SIDE OF CHOPOK !!! **  
If you book accommodation on the northern side in Demänovská dolina, you will have to drive approximately 80 km by car in the morning to reach the start line, as the cable cars do not run that early!

##### **Getting to the start : **  

**Airports**  
The most easily accessible airports by public transport are:  
Poprad Airport (TAT) - Bus or train to Brezno, multiple times a day, around 2-3h  
Košice Airport (KSC) - Trains and direct buses to Brezno, around 2.5-4h  
Bratislava Airport (BTS) - Bus or train to Brezno, including direct connections, around 4h  
Vienna Airport (VIE) - Bus to Bratislava, then same as BTS.   
Budapest Airport (BUD) - Direct FlixBus from Budapest to Banská Bystrica multiple times a day.  

Other airports such as KRK and KTW are also fairly close but are not very well connected by public transport. Car rental is recommended in those cases.  

Banská Bystrica is a bigger city that is very well connected with Brezno (bus/train every 30-60 min).  
On the opposite side, Liptovský Mikuláš looks close to Brezno, but only about 2 buses per day connect them.  
Poprad is somewhere in between, it is connected, but it usually takes longer to get there.  

For finding bus and train connections we use [cp.sk](https://cp.sk/vlakbusmhd/spojenie)

**Getting to start:**  
You can reach Hotel Srdiečko via the bus line Brezno - Mýto pod Ďumbierom - Bystrá - Tále - Srdiečko.  
This bus runs approximately 4 times a day. However, it will not get you to the start in time, as the schedule starts too late in the morning. 

**Accomodation**  
We strongly recommend staying at Hotel Srdiečko***, as it is the main venue for the entire event, located right by the start area and registration desk.  
There are also more accomodations available around 1-1.5km from start. After that there is just around 6km of road with forest.  
Other accomodations are available at Tále (around 7km from start) and then Bystrá/Mýto pod Ďumbierom (12km from start).  

**Parking**:  
There are approximately 80 parking spaces in the paid parking lot at Hotel Srdiečko. Unofficially, it is also possible to park for free along the roadside.  
Within one kilometer (and approx. 100 meters of elevation gain) from the start, there are also the paid parking lots Krupová and Trangoška, which together offer over 500 spaces. However, the parking lot at Srdiečko should be sufficient.
