### **Skymarathon Chopok and Skyrace Jasná**
_Discover your limits!
_

 **Leitner Sport** invites all mountain runners to the Low Tatras for a marathon and half marathon.
The race starts on the southern side of Chopok at Srdiečko, and runners will cover perhaps all the most beautiful hiking trails on both sides of Chopok. The final climb from Biela púť in Jasná, through Tri vody, Luková, up to Chopok will test every competitor.
The finish line for both routes is at Chopok (at an altitude of 2,000 m a.s.l.). The total elevation gain of the marathon route is 2,600 m and the half marathon route is 1,400 m.

Relay teams can also enter the marathon route, with each team member covering a section of approximately 10 km. The number of members in a relay team can be 4, 3, or 2. However, an exchange of team members must always take place at the handover point. Relay team members travel to individual sections by cable car.

The event is physically demanding. It is very beneficial for runners to have experience in mountain running.

**The event is included in the UTMB and ITRA calendar for 2026. **
